# ScannerMApp privacy policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

ScannerMApp is a QR code and barcode scanner. It is open source: everything described here can be checked in its [source code](https://github.com/andrea-magni/ScannerMApp).

## In short

ScannerMApp does not collect, send or share any data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and on Android it does not even have the permission to access the internet.

## Camera

The app uses the camera only while you are scanning, after you tap **Scan**, and asks for the permission the first time. The frames of the camera are decoded on the device and are not saved, except the frame in which a code was read: it is kept with that scan, so that you can see it in the result card.

## Pictures

When you pick a picture from your photo library, share one or more pictures with ScannerMApp, or open a picture with it, the app reads the codes in it on the device. It receives only the pictures you choose; it does not need, and does not ask for, the permission to read your storage.

## Scans

The scans (the content of the codes, their format, the date, how many times you scanned them and the frame in which they were read) and your settings (the formats to read) are saved in the private storage of the app on your device. They are not sent anywhere. You can delete a scan from the app at any time; uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of them.

## Opening, copying and sharing

When you tap **Open** on a scan, the app hands its content (a link, a phone number, an e-mail, a message, a place, a contact or an event) to the app of your device that handles it, such as the browser, the dialer, the mail app, the maps, the contacts or the calendar. When you tap **Copy** or **Share**, the content goes to the clipboard or to the app you choose. From that moment, what happens to it depends on that app. ScannerMApp does this only when you tap the button.

## Children

ScannerMApp does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address, with its date.
